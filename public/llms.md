# Shieldmaiden

> Free D&D 5e combat tracker and DM companion app. Built by HarmlessKey,
> a two-person team in Amsterdam. Public since February 2019.

Core features are free forever. Paid tiers (via Patreon) add storage and
quality-of-life features. Content is SRD 5.1 only, under OGL 1.0a.
A demo encounter runs without an account.

## Tools
- [Combat Tracker](https://shieldmaiden.app/tools/combat-tracker): initiative,
  HP, conditions, concentration reminders, damage types, combat log with undo,
  multi-targeting, shared live initiative list for players.
- [Encounter Builder](https://shieldmaiden.app/tools/encounter-builder): build
  encounters with SRD or custom monsters, instant difficulty calculation.
- [Monster Creator](https://shieldmaiden.app/tools/monster-creator): custom
  statblocks with rollable actions.
- [Spell Creator](https://shieldmaiden.app/tools/spell-creator)
- [Character Sync](https://shieldmaiden.app/tools/character-sync): import from
  D&D Beyond.
